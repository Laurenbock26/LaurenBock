About Me

Hi! My name is Lauren Bock, and I am a senior studying Marketing at Iowa State University. I am passionate about marketing, fashion, social media, and creating meaningful experiences for consumers.

Throughout my college experience, I have developed skills in communication, teamwork, customer service, creativity, and problem solving. My coursework has allowed me to explore different areas of marketing, including consumer behavior, professional selling, business communication, and digital marketing.

My Experience

One of the most meaningful experiences during my college career was studying abroad in Florence, Italy. Living abroad allowed me to experience a new culture, meet people from around the world, and become more independent.

I also had the opportunity to travel throughout Europe and experience new places, foods, and cultures. These experiences helped me become more confident, adaptable, and open-minded.

Career Goals

I am especially interested in pursuing a career related to fashion merchandising, fashion marketing, digital marketing, or social media. I enjoy combining creativity with marketing strategy to connect brands with their target audiences.

My goal is to use my marketing knowledge, creativity, communication skills, and passion for fashion and digital media in a professional environment where I can continue learning and growing.
